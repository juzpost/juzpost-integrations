# Make listing copy

**App name:** JuzPost

**Tagline:** Schedule and publish social media posts to 11 platforms from one calendar.

**Description:**

JuzPost is a social media scheduler for creators, small teams and agencies. Plan a month of content in one calendar and publish to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Threads, Bluesky, Tumblr and WordPress. The Make app lets a scenario upload media, create drafts, schedule or publish them to up to ten accounts, spread a batch of drafts over preset posting times, and react when a post is published or fails. Free plan with low-cost paid plans.

**Example scenarios for the listing page:**

2. Row added in Google Sheets, create a text post, publish now to X, Threads and Bluesky.
3. Post failed in JuzPost, send a Slack message with the error.
